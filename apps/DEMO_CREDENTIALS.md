## FEAR public app

Sign in with any 10-digit Indian mobile number. In dev mode the OTP is printed in the fear_backend console/log (`[fear][dev-otp]`) and listed under **Dev OTP outbox** for the super admin.

## Phones by QR code

In the red-lights GUI (http://127.0.0.1:8765/simulation.html), sign in to the FEAR Command panel as `cmd-iccc` (or `fear-admin` / an org admin) and use **Phones -> Add phone**. The phone app's **Scan QR** connects to this server and signs in - no typing. QR codes are single-use and expire in 30 minutes.
